            How rooms work

            Start a room, share the code, and everyone plays the same puzzles at the same second.

            Say you are ready. Once everybody has, the host kicks off a five second countdown, and the words are only picked when it hits zero — so nobody gets an early look, host included.

            Every game has a clock on it, so one stuck player cannot hold everyone up. You score on how few guesses you needed first and how fast you were second: a tidy solve always beats a scrappy quick one.

            While you play you can see who has finished and how many guesses they have spent. You never see what anyone actually guessed.

            Lose your connection and come back, and you pick up exactly where you were. Two players make it count towards the room table — on your own it is just practice.
