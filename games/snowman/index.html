How to play

Snowman is a free daily word game — hangman by another name. Call out letters to find the hidden word before the snowman melts away, with only its category to go on. A new word every day, and unlimited words whenever you want more.

Same rules as hangman, with a snowman in the middle.

Pick letters to uncover the hidden word. Every letter that is not in there melts a bit more of the snowman — six wrong and all that is left is a puddle.

The category above the word is your only clue. Spaces are filled in already, so all you ever have to guess is letters.

p la r e ar

A right letter shows up everywhere it appears — one A in polar bear gets you both.

Tapping a letter you already tried costs you nothing. That is a slip, not a guess.

Think you have it? Type the whole word in the box. Get it wrong and it costs you a life, same as a wrong letter.

Today, and the rest

Today is one word for everybody, everywhere, and it is the only one that counts towards your record and your streak. It changes at midnight New York time.

Feel like more? Unlimited deals another word whenever you want one, as often as you like. Your streak is safe there: nothing you do in Unlimited can end it.

You do not pick the difficulty any more, and Unlimited is the gentler of the two. The hardest words in the book are kept for the daily one.

No word comes back until you have seen every other one at its level.
