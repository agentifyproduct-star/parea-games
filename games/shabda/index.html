How to play

Shabda is a free daily word game. Guess the hidden word in six tries on a four, five or six letter board — every correct letter lights up, and everyone in the world gets the same word each day.

Find the hidden word in 6 guesses. Today is one word for everybody, on a board of 4, 5 or 6 letters — which one changes day to day.

After every guess the tiles change colour to show how close you got.

Any real word counts as a guess. If we do not know it, we look it up before holding it against you — random letters will just get handed back.

Today's word is the only one that counts towards your record and streak. The 4, 5 and 6 buttons deal practice words as often as you like, and those count for nothing. A new day starts at midnight New York time.
