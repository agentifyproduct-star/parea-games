How to play

Shabda is a free daily word game. Guess the hidden word in six tries on a board of four to seven letters — every correct letter lights up, and everyone in the world gets the same word each day.

Find the hidden word in 6 guesses. Today is one word for everybody, on a board of 4 to 7 letters. Which one changes day to day, and you do not choose it.

After every guess the tiles change colour to show how close you got.

Any real word counts as a guess. If we do not know it, we look it up before holding it against you — random letters will just get handed back.

Today's word is the one that counts towards your record and streak. Unlimited deals another word whenever you want one, on a board drawn the same way. Your streak is safe there: nothing you do in Unlimited can end it. A new day starts at midnight New York time.
