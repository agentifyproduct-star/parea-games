About

Parea Games is a free daily word game site with three games — Shabda, Snowman and Anagram. Everyone in the world gets the same puzzles each day, there is nothing to install and no account to make.

Three games, no ads, no accounts, nothing to download and nobody watching what you type. Open it and play — that is the whole idea.

Your streaks and stats stay on whatever you play on, so your phone and your laptop keep separate records. Everyone gets the same puzzles each day, and they change at midnight New York time wherever in the world you are.

Questions

The ones people actually ask

What is Parea Games?

Parea Games is a free word game site with three daily games: Shabda, Snowman and Anagram. Everyone in the world gets the same puzzles each day, so whoever else played today played exactly what you played.

Is it free, and do I need an account?

It is free and there is no account. Nothing to install, no ads, and no sign-up — open the page and play. Your streaks are kept in your own browser, which means your phone and your laptop keep separate records.

When does the new puzzle arrive?

At midnight New York time, and at that same instant everywhere. A player in London, Mumbai or Sydney gets the new puzzles at the same moment as a player in New York, just at a different hour of their own day.

How do I play Shabda?

Guess the hidden word in six tries. Each guess has to be a real word; letters in the right place turn green, letters in the word but the wrong place turn amber, and letters that are not in the word go grey. You can play a four, five or six letter board.

How do I play Snowman?

Call out one letter at a time to uncover a hidden word, knowing only its category. Every wrong letter melts away a piece of the snowman, and you have six wrong guesses before he is a puddle.

How do I play Anagram?

One scrambled word arrives each day, between five and eight letters long. Unscramble it within five tries. Everyone gets the same word, so the day is the same puzzle for everybody, and a hint is there after three wrong tries if you want it.
